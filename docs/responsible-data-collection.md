# Responsible data collection

- **Checked before quoting.** robots.txt, the site's terms and the kind of data involved
  are reviewed during the pre-check, and any concerns are raised in writing.
- **Personal data.** Where names, emails or phone numbers are collected, we agree the
  lawful basis first (POPIA, GDPR or the law that applies to you), and collect only what
  the purpose needs.
- **Polite by default.** Requests are paced per domain, concurrency is limited, and jobs
  identify themselves sensibly. Sites are never overloaded.
- **No breaking in.** I don't bypass captchas, paywalls or logins you don't have the
  right to use. Logged-in collection only uses accounts you own and are allowed to
  automate.
- **Nothing invented.** If data cannot be recovered or verified, the delivery says so;
  it is never filled in or estimated silently.
- **Traceable.** Every delivery includes a manifest of sources, times and hashes.
